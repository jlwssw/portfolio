About

Statistics, code, and a bias for real-world impact

I'm an early-career data scientist with a B.S. in Mathematics and Ph.D. study in Computer Science. My work sits at the intersection of applied statistics, Python, and modern AI — training predictive models, building LLM-powered applications, and translating messy, large-scale data into insights people can act on.

I taught AP Statistics for six years, so hypothesis testing, regression, and inference are second nature. In my current research I analyze large-scale education data, build AI tools with Hugging Face and the Anthropic API, and collaborate with cross-functional teams — always keeping non-technical stakeholders in mind.

I'm most energized by open-ended problems: turning a fuzzy question into a testable hypothesis, pulling the right data, and shipping a model or analysis that changes a decision.
